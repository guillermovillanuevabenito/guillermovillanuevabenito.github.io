Neural oscillatory patterns can be characterized by attributes such as frequency, amplitude, duty cycle and number of spikes per burst. Multiple combinations of parameters can generate patterns with the same attributes, which gives neurons and networks flexibility, and presents modelers with unidentifiability in parameter estimation. This thesis describes a canonical model for oscillation level sets in single cells and studies whether, and under what circumstances, these level sets are conserved in the networks in which the cells are embedded.
